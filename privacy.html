Pet Hub privacy policy

Pet Hub is an app by Marvel Corp, part of the Hub family of apps. Last updated November 13, 2026.

The short version

What you put in Pet Hub stays on your phone. There are no Pet Hub accounts, no ad trackers and no analytics in the app. Your information is never sold and never used for advertising. One optional AI feature sends some of it to an AI service to answer you - only when you use it - and it is listed below.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type: your first name if you give it, and for each pet its name, species, breed, colour, boy or girl, birthday or estimated age, Gotcha Day, the colour you pick for it, its daily care routine and what you tick off, medicines as you typed them from your vet and the doses you log, shots and when they are due, vet visits and notes, weights, memories and their stories, the passport details (microchip and tag numbers, vet and emergency vet names and phone numbers, insurance company and policy number, notes for a sitter) and the day a pet passed if you use the Rainbow Bridge page. These are saved in your browser's storage on your phone. We cannot see them.

Photos (profile photos, memory photos and pictures of papers such as vaccine records) are kept in a separate store on your phone. They are never put in the backup and never sent anywhere.

Deleting: ⚙ Settings → Your data → Erase everything deletes your Pet Hub information on the phone (photos included). Removing one pet: open its profile → Edit → Remove this pet.

Not veterinary advice

Pet Hub keeps your own records. It never works out a dose or tells you what treatment to use; medicines show exactly what you typed from your vet. General tips (like life stages) are general information only - ask your vet.
